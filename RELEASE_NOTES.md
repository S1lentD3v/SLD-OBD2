## SLD OBD2 v1.0.0 — first release

Ad-free OBD2 diagnostics that explains your car in plain English.

### Highlights
- **Trouble codes** with plain-English explanations, severity and freeze-frame data; clear codes; scan history; PDF report.
- **Check all modules** — airbag, ABS, body, climate and more, with the type of fault.
- **Live data** — gauges, graphs and CSV data logging.
- **Manufacturer extras** — extra make/model-specific readings (EV battery, tyre pressures, wheel speeds…).
- **Listen mode** — reads your car's own network traffic without sending anything.
- **Vehicle info** — VIN, emissions readiness, detailed test results, DPF health.
- **Trucks & buses** (J1939) — warning lamps and active fault codes.
- Bluetooth (classic + LE), Wi-Fi and USB ELM327 adapters; built-in demo car to try it without an adapter.

### Install
Download **SLD-OBD2-v1.0.0.apk** below, open it on your phone and allow installing from this source if asked. Requires Android 7.0+.
